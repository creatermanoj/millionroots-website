35 Years of Service
The Story Behind the Mission

A farmer's son who fed a nation

"Before I retire, I want to plant one lakh trees."

Shri G. Sivannarayana dedicated 35 years of his life as Project Director at ATMA — the Agricultural Technology Management Agency — transforming farming communities across Andhra Pradesh.

With an MSc in Agriculture and a lifetime of quiet service, his retirement wish was simple: to give back to the land that gave so much to so many.

On July 30, 2026 — his retirement day — we're not just fulfilling that wish. We're scaling it tenfold. One million native trees, planted in his honour.

What We Do

Three roots of one mission

Every tree we plant, every farmer we train, every child we inspire — all in service of a greener, more resilient Andhra Pradesh.

🌳

Afforestation

Planting one million native trees across Andhra Pradesh — from Nellore to the Northern districts — restoring biodiversity and sequestering carbon for generations to come.

1,000,000 trees · Native species
🌱

Organic Farming

A demonstration farm near Nellore — growing organic produce, running a plant nursery, and proving that sustainable agriculture is both possible and profitable.

Demo farm · Nellore, AP
🤝

Community

Training farmers, partnering with corporates on CSR tree-planting drives, and taking the mission into schools — growing the next generation of environmental stewards.

Volunteers · CSR · Schools
